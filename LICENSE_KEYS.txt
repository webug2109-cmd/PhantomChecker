3. HOW TO GENERATE A NEW LICENSE KEY FOR ANY MACHINE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
When a user launches Phantom Checker on a new machine for the first
time, the app displays their unique 64-character Machine ID.

To generate their activation key:

On Windows:
  cd dist-keygen
  phantom-keygen.exe --id <64-HEX-MACHINE-ID>

On Linux:
  chmod +x dist-keygen/phantom-keygen-linux
  ./dist-keygen/phantom-keygen-linux --id <64-HEX-MACHINE-ID>

With Node.js:
  node keygen/keygen.cjs --id <64-HEX-MACHINE-ID>

Example:
  phantom-keygen.exe --id 6e9387735fe4d24825555eae745185604fc112a59fa3a3943ab763c39ed1727e
  Output: PHANTOM-D010D36D-B8A92128-79A4C216-E14BD06E
